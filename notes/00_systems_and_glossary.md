# Systems we use and common terms

## Software and systems
- **Jetpack Workflow ("Jetpack")** - where we track every job, its label/status, its steps, and who is assigned.
- **Onvio** - client portal and document storage. Client documents, the client ID, and client phone numbers (client dashboard) are here.
- **SurePrep** - where client source documents are organized into "binders" for 1040 prep (scan, organize, verify).
- **UltraTax** - our tax preparation software.
- **SafeSend** - how returns are delivered to clients for e-signature. The "delivered returns" list shows who still needs to sign.
- **QuickBooks Online (QBO)** - bookkeeping for clients.
- **Synovus** - bank used for ACH collections, recipients and checks (see the Synovus note).

## Terms and abbreviations
- **ZZ on Jetpack** - the client is no longer a client.
- **PY** - prior year (e.g., "PY return").
- **ACH** - electronic bank payment / draft.
- **PPD / CCD** - ACH SEC codes: PPD = a person, CCD = a company.
- **JE** - journal entry.
- **DR / CR** - debit / credit.
- **A/R** - accounts receivable.
- **Superseded** (in SurePrep review) - the file was replaced because there were duplicates.
- **1040** - individual tax return.
- **Extensions** - extensions of time to file tax returns. When pulling documents for a SurePrep binder, do NOT pull the client's extensions.
- **HSA (Health Savings Account)** - an account the client can put money into pre-tax.
  - **Distributions** = what the client takes out of the account.
  - HSA forms 1099-SA and 5498-SA go to Form 8889 (Health Savings Account) in UltraTax.
