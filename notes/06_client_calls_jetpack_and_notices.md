# Calling clients, new potential clients, and tax notices

## Calling clients to e-sign their returns
1. Find the list in **SafeSend** under **Delivered returns.**
2. Find the client's phone number in **Onvio** under the client dashboard.
3. Script: "I was calling to remind you to please e-sign your tax return whenever you get a chance."

## Calling clients who haven't sent their engagement letter / organizer
Use on a **case-by-case basis.** Script:
- "I was calling because we prepared your tax return last year and are planning to prepare your 2025 return, but it looks like we do not have your signed engagement letter and completed organizer."
- "We also need you to please upload your source documents so we can prepare your return."
- "We are just trying to make sure we have enough time to prepare your return before the extension deadline."
- "If you have made other plans, we totally understand. We are just trying to plan our capacity for the rest of the season."

## Adding a potential new client in Jetpack
1. **Jetpack → Add client** → first and last name only.
2. Label: **"Potential client - tax"**, and put in the year they are asking about.
3. **New job → Assign template → "Potential new client".**
4. **In date** = the date they called.
5. Check off steps 1 and 2.
6. Leave a note.

## Tax notices
- Always put a **due date** on a tax notice so it doesn't get lost track of.

## Jetpack reminders
- **ZZ** on a Jetpack client means they are no longer a client.
