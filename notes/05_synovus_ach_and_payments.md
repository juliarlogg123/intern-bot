# Synovus (bank): ACH collections, recipients, and checks

## Getting quarterly payments (ACH collection)
- **Payment → Make payment → ACH collection → SEC Code drop-down:**
  - **PPD** for a person
  - **CCD** for a company

## Adding a new recipient
1. **Add recipient** → put the name you want to show at the top.
2. Select **checking or savings.**
3. Enter the **account #** and **routing #** (the routing number goes in the Financial Institutions box on the left) → click the blue check.
4. Scroll down → **ACH name:** put the person's whole name exactly as it appears on the form.
5. **Save recipient.**

## Adding an invoice / writing a check
1. **Create → Check.**
2. Enter who you paid.
3. Choose the category and add a description.
4. Enter the amount on the far right.
5. **Save and close.**
6. Scan both the check and the invoice and put them into Onvio.

## Verifying against QuickBooks
- **Transactions → Activity center →** open the payment details to verify against QuickBooks.

## After the draft goes through
1. In QBO, **record a payment for each individual.** Select **ACH**, **Undeposited Funds**, and use the date of the draft.
2. **Create → Bank deposit →** select everyone.
3. When done, go to **Accounting → Bank transactions → Match.**

## Journal entries behind this process
1. When dues are billed: **DR A/R / CR Dues**
2. When the payment is recorded: **DR Undeposited Funds / CR A/R**
3. When the deposit hits the bank: **DR Synovus / CR Undeposited Funds**
